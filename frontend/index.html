What is ExamLens AI?

ExamLens AI (also known as Exam Lens AI or examlens-m4ji) transforms paper-based university and school exam grading. Using state-of-the-art vision models and precision handwriting OCR, ExamLens ingests handwritten exam scripts, extracts student answers question by question, applies rigorous rubric grading criteria, and generates instant diagnostic feedback.

📄 Physical Exam OCR

Ingests multi-page scanned student answer sheets with intelligent handwriting recognition and noise cleanup.

⚖️ Rubric-Based Scoring

Evaluates key concepts, formulas, derivations, and final steps with partial credit scoring.

📊 Instant Analytics

Generates cohort mastery heatmaps, student score distributions, and exportable grade reports.
